About

Project Halophyte is a collaboration between UNSW Sydney's Water Research Laboratory and the University of the South Pacific, funded by Swire Shipping. It spans tidal restoration, Mangrove-Integrated Coastal Infrastructure (MICI) and research on societal impact.

MICI combines modular floating pontoons with planted mangroves. Each module carries a lined planter sized for one mature mangrove, with a deck on top so people can still use the structure. The long-term aim is a longer version that can be installed in marinas in Fiji and Australia.
