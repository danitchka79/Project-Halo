Project basis

Pontoon flotation & stability

Rectilinear unit, metacentric method of Appendix A. Stability load applied over half the deck width (worst eccentricity), flotation load over the full deck.

Channels, berths & depths

Minimum and preferred dimensions from the design vessel. Beam and draft default from Tables 3.1 and 3.2 (95th percentile); override with surveyed fleet data.

Harbour wave climate

Enter significant wave heights inside the basin for each direction and period class. Leave blank where the condition does not occur.

Wind, current & berthing loads

Horizontal design actions per berth pair and per metre of structure, combined with the suggested strength factors of Cl. 4.2.

Pontoon restraint: guide piles or chains

Holds the floating walkway itself in place (not the piles between boats, which are on the Vessel mooring tab). Takes the design horizontal load for a pontoon section (from the Loads tab, or entered by hand) and checks two restraint options: cantilever guide piles, and chains to gravity blocks.

Vessel mooring: lines, cleats & intermediate piles

Beam-on wind and current on a berthed vessel, line tension, cleat provision, and the freestanding mooring pile that separates boats in a double berth.

Gangway, walkways & access

Slope from geometry at three water levels, checked against Table 3.5; widths from Tables 3.3 and 3.4.

Services, safety & amenities

Firefighting, life-safety equipment, power, water and amenity counts from the berth numbers. AS 3962 Section 6 and SRSA Sections 10–12 side by side.

Boat ramps

Public or marina launching ramps for trailered boats: gradient, toe depth, lane width, manoeuvring space, shelter and parking (AS 3962 Section 7, SRSA 10.7).

Offshore aquaculture

Design criteria for floating fish farms from classification rules (DNV, ABS, BV) and national standards (NS 9415, Marine Scotland, ISO 16488), as reviewed by Chu et al. (2023): design life, current, wind, net drag with shielding, load combinations, marine growth and mooring safety factors.

Project checklist

Whole-of-project requirements from approvals through site investigation, modelling, design, landside facilities and utilities to commissioning. Based on SRSA Appendix I (minimum requirements for an SRSA permit) and SRSA Sections 1–11, with matching AS 3962 clauses. Ticks are saved in this browser.
